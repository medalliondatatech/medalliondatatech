Our Story

Empowering Businesses Through Technology & Talent

MedallionDataTech Private Limited was founded with a clear purpose: to be the technology and talent partner that businesses across India and the world can rely on. Headquartered in Hyderabad — India's premier tech hub — we combine deep domain expertise with a client-centric approach.

We serve a diverse clientele spanning startups, SMEs, and large enterprises, delivering IT services, outsourcing solutions, SaaS products, and end-to-end human resource services. Our professionals bring experience across fintech, healthcare, retail, logistics, and more.

At MedallionDataTech, we believe that technology is only as powerful as the people behind it — which is why our staffing and recruitment services are an integral part of who we are.
